The ageing of lithium-ion batteries exhibits voltage characteristics similar to those of ISC.

However, there are significant differences in the nature of these two types of failure, and misdiagnosis may pose safety risks.

 Therefore, the accurate identification of lithium-ion battery failures is of great engineering significance.

To address this issue, this study proposes a machine learning-based diagnostic method that analyzes features extracted from IC curves to achieve the identification of aging, ISC faults, and their coexisting conditions.
